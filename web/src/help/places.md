A place is somewhere you go: home, work, the store, the gym. When Ah-Ha knows you're there, it shows the things you tagged to that place.

## Add a place

In the phone app, open ⚙️ **Settings**, then 📍 **Places**. Stand at the place, then use **Add a place where you are now** and give it a name and an icon.

## Automatic check-in

With **Auto check-in** on, the phone notices when you arrive at a place and checks you in. You get a notification of what's tagged there. When you leave, you're checked out.

The phone has to be allowed to use your location for this. You can change the size of the area around a place in 📍 **Places**.

## Check in by hand

You can also check in yourself, or with an [[nfc-tags|NFC tag]] by the door.

## Tag things to a place

On a list item or task, choose **📍 Where?** and pick the place. Then:

- Ah! Today shows it under the place's name while you're there.
- The [[heading-out|Heading out]] checklist shows it when you're going there.
- [[ah-cart|Ah! Cart]] picks the store you're at automatically.
