This page is the short version. The full details are in the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## On your phone only

- **Ah! Rhythm** keeps your items, doses and times on your phone. If you connect it, what you take or do is also written to your Ah-Ha trail.
- **Ah! Cart** keeps its trips and remembered prices on your phone. If you connect it, trips are also written to your Ah-Ha trail.

## In your Ah-Ha account

Your lists, tasks, notes, places and trail live in your Ah-Ha account, so they're the same on the web and on your phone.

## What the helpers read

The [[helpers|helpers]] only read your own account: your lists, tasks, snoozes and trail. They don't share it with other users.

## What goes to Anthropic

A few features use Anthropic's Claude to write a sentence for you:

- **The briefing** on Ah! Today sends the things it summarizes, such as task titles, upcoming events and the place you're at.
- **Follow-through** sends the title of an item you've snoozed three times, to suggest a first step.

If you add your own Anthropic key in Settings, your key is used instead of ours.
