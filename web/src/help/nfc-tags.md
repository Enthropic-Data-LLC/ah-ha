An NFC tag is a small sticker your phone can read when you hold it close. Put one where you do something, and tap your phone on it to record it in one move.

**Ideas:** on the pill bottle ("took my meds"), by the front door (check in at home, or open the Heading out checklist), on the washing machine.

## Make a tag

You need a blank NFC sticker. They're cheap online.

1. In the Ah-Ha phone app, open ⚙️ **Settings**, then 🏷️ **NFC tags**, and tap **+ New**.
2. Give it a name, for example "Meds".
3. Choose what happens **when tapped**: write to your trail, check in at a place, tick off a task, or open Heading out.
4. Tap **Create & write to tag**, then hold the phone on the sticker until it says **✓ Written**.

NFC has to be switched on in your phone's settings.

## Use it

Hold the phone on the tag. That's it. A second tap within 30 seconds is ignored, so a double tap doesn't count twice.

## With Ah! Rhythm

If [[ah-rhythm|Ah! Rhythm]] is connected to your Ah-Ha, a tag that mentions meds, pills, vitamins or 💊 also ticks things off there. Name a time in the tag ("Bedtime vitamins") and it ticks off everything at that time. See [[ah-rhythm|Ah! Rhythm]].
