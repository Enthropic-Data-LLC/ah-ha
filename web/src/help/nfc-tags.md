A tag does one thing when you use it: logs something, checks you in at a place, or opens a checklist. Put it where that thing happens, and it takes one move.

Every tag works two ways:

- as an **NFC sticker**: hold your phone close to it
- as a printed **QR code**: point your phone's camera at it

Use whichever suits you. You can have both for the same tag.

**Ideas:** on the pill bottle ("took my meds"), by the front door (check in at home, or open the Heading out checklist), on the washing machine.

## Make a tag and print its QR code

On a computer is easiest:

1. Sign in at ah-ha.app and open **Tags**.
2. Under **Make a tag**, give it an icon and a name, for example 💊 "Meds".
3. Choose what happens **when scanned**: log something, check in at a place, open Heading out, or open Ah! Today.
4. Tap **Make tag**. Its QR code appears in **Your tags**.
5. Tap **Print**. Or tick several and **Print selected** to get them on one sheet. **Download PNG** gives you an image for a label printer.

Print on sticker paper, or print and tape it up. Keep each code at least 3 cm (1¼ in) wide.

In the phone app you can also tap **QR** next to a tag to show its code or print it.

## Write it to an NFC sticker

You need a blank NFC sticker. They're cheap online.

1. In the Ah-Ha phone app, open ⚙️ **Settings**, then 🏷️ **NFC tags**, and tap **+ New** (or **Write** next to a tag you already made).
2. Give it a name, for example "Meds".
3. Choose what happens **when tapped**: write to your trail, check in at a place, tick off a task, or open Heading out.
4. Tap **Create & write to tag**, then hold the phone on the sticker until it says **✓ Written**.

NFC has to be switched on in your phone's settings.

## Use it

- **NFC:** hold the phone on the sticker.
- **QR code:** open your phone's camera and point it at the code. With the Ah-Ha app installed, it runs straight away. Without it, the tag's page opens in your browser: tap the button there to run it. Sign in first if it asks.

Using a tag twice within 30 seconds only counts once, so a double tap or a slow scan doesn't count twice.

## With Ah! Rhythm

If [[ah-rhythm|Ah! Rhythm]] is connected to your Ah-Ha, a tag (NFC or QR) that mentions meds, pills, vitamins or 💊 also ticks things off there. Name a time in the tag ("Bedtime vitamins") and it ticks off everything at that time. See [[ah-rhythm|Ah! Rhythm]].
