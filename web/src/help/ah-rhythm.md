Ah! Rhythm helps you keep a daily rhythm: what to take, what to do, and when. It reminds you at the right time and lets you tick off a whole group at once.

> **Important:** Ah! Rhythm is a helpful reminder, not a medical device. Don't make it your only reminder for a prescription medicine.

## Kinds of items

When you add an item, choose what kind it is:

- **Take:** a supplement or medicine, with how to take it (with food, a full glass of water…).
- **Do:** something to do, like a walk or a workout, with a target ("30 min").
- **Window:** a stretch of time, like an eating window from 11 to 7, or no screens after 10.
- **Track:** a 1–10 rating you give in the morning and evening, for example how much pain you're in.

## Named times

Instead of a clock time, give items a **named time**: Wake-up, Breakfast, Midday, Dinner or Bedtime. Everything at the same named time:

- shows together on Today, under one heading ("Breakfast · 7:30 AM")
- gets **one** reminder
- moves together if you change that time

To set your times, open **Items → Times** and tap a time to change it.

### Already have items with clock times?

On the **Times** screen, under **Group my times**, each clock time your items use is listed with the nearest named time picked for you. Change any that are wrong, then tap **Group them**.

## Tick off a whole group

You never have to tick items one at a time:

- On Today, tap **Take all** next to a time.
- On the reminder, tap **Took all**.
- Or use an [[nfc-tags|NFC tag or QR code]] (Ah! Rhythm needs to be connected to Ah-Ha for this). A tag naming a time ("Bedtime vitamins") ticks off that time. A tag that names nothing ticks off the group nearest to now.

If you're connected to Ah-Ha, each group also shows on [[ah-today|Ah! Today]], and ticking it there counts in Ah! Rhythm too.

## Reminders

A reminder comes at the time, with **Taken**, **15 min** (remind me again) and **Skip**. If you don't answer, it reminds you once more an hour later.

## Stop missed times piling up

If you miss a time, it can stay on your list all day next to the later ones. To stop that, edit the item and choose **Time out if not done**: at the next time, or after 1, 2 or 4 hours. A timed-out time shows as **Missed**, stops reminding you, and you can still tap **Took it** if you did.

## Running low

Add how many you have and Ah! Rhythm warns you when you're running low. If you're connected, it adds "Refill …" to your Ah-Ha list.
