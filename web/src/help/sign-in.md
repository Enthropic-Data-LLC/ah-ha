Ah-Ha has no passwords. You sign in with a link sent to your email.

## How to sign in

1. Go to **ah-ha.app** and tap **Sign in**.
2. Type your email address and tap the button.
3. Open the email from Ah-Ha and tap the link in it.
4. On the page that opens, tap the button to finish signing in.

The first time, you choose a username. It appears in the addresses of your pages.

> **Tip:** The link works once and only for a short while. If it has expired, ask for a new one.

## Didn't get the email?

- Look in your spam or junk folder.
- Wait a minute and try again. Check the address for typos.
