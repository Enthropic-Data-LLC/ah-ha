Ah! Cart adds up your shopping as it goes in the cart, sales tax included, so you know what you'll pay before you reach the register. It's made for shopping with the cash you have on you.

## Start a trip

1. Open Ah! Cart and enter **Cash I have**.
2. Check the **Sales tax rate** for where you are. You can also add the store's name (optional).
3. Tap **Start shopping**.

## Add things

Tap **+ Add item** as things go in the cart. Choose **Each** with a price, or **By the pound** with a price per pound. The total at the top tells you what you'll pay and what you have left.

Ah! Cart remembers what you've bought before, so next time it suggests the item and its last price as you type.

Tap an item in the cart to change it or **Remove** it.

## Shop from your list

If you [[connect-apps|connect]] Ah! Cart to Ah-Ha, the **To get** tab shows your shopping list. Tap an item to add it to the cart, and it's ticked off on your list.

If you're checked in at a store, Ah! Cart picks it for you and shows what you tagged for that store first.

Items that [[helpers|Restock]] put back on your list show why underneath ("↺ You buy this about every 9 days").

## When you're done

Tap **Reset**, then **Save & reset**. The trip is saved under **Trips** on your phone. If you're connected, it's also logged to Ah-Ha, which helps Restock learn what you buy and how often.
