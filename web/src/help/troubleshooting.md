## "App not installed" when updating

The new version was signed differently from the one on your phone, so Android won't put one over the other.

- **Ah-Ha:** it's safe to uninstall it and install the new one. Your information is in your Ah-Ha account. Afterwards, paste your key again (see [[connect-apps|Connect an app]]).
- **Ah! Rhythm or Ah! Cart:** **don't uninstall.** Their information lives only on your phone, and uninstalling erases it. Contact us first and we'll help you move it.

## The Android page shows only some apps

Your browser is showing an old copy of the page. Pull down to refresh, or open it in a new tab.

## Ah! Today takes a while to open

When you open it, it may write you a fresh briefing, which can take a few seconds. The rest of the screen fills in on the next refresh.

## Reminders arrive late

Android may be holding them back to save battery. In Ah! Rhythm, open ⚙️ settings. If it says reminders may run late, tap **Allow exact times**.

## The sign-in email doesn't arrive

Look in spam or junk, then ask for a new link. See [[sign-in|Sign in]].

## Ah! Cart doesn't show my shopping list

- Check that it's connected: **Pair with Ah-Ha** should say **Connected to…**.
- Tap **↻ Sync**.
- Check that the list's name in Ah! Cart matches your shopping list in Ah-Ha.
