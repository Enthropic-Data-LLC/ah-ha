Ah! Today (in full, Ah-Ha Today) is the first thing you see in the app and at **ah-ha.app/now**. It gathers what matters today from all your lists and boards, so you don't have to look through each one.

## Focus now

At the top is **one thing**: what Ah! Today thinks deserves your attention right now, with a few words on why ("overdue", "due 7:50 AM", "you're at the store").

- Tap **Done** when it's finished.
- Tap **Not now** to put it aside for two hours. It comes back later.

## The rest of your day

Below the focus you'll see, when there's something in them:

- **Needs attention:** things that are overdue.
- **Now:** habits for this time of day.
- **Today:** things due today.
- **Pick up** or the name of a place: things you tagged to where you are now. See [[places|Places]].
- **Just resurfaced:** things you put off that are back.

## Ticking things off on your phone

Rows are locked so a stray swipe can't clear anything.

1. Tap a row to unlock it.
2. Swipe right to mark it done, or tap ✓.
3. On tasks, swipe left (or tap ⏸) to snooze.

## Tell it how you are

Under the header there are small chips. Tap the energy chip to say how you are today: low, normal or good energy. Ah! Today then picks easier or harder things to match, until tonight. You can also mark a **day off**.

## Morning and evening

- **The briefing** is a sentence or two about your day, written fresh when your place or the time of day changes.
- **The evening wrap-up** shows what you got done today.
- **Your streak** counts the days in a row you got at least one thing done.

## Things you keep putting off

If you snooze something three times, Ah! Today brings it forward with a small first step to get you going. See [[helpers|Helpers]].
