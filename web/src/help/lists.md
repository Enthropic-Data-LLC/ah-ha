A list is a simple checklist: a shopping list, things to pack, errands.

## Make a list and add items

1. On ah-ha.app, open your spaces and make a new **List**.
2. Type an item and tap **Add**.
3. Tick the box when it's done.

On the phone, use the bar at the bottom of Ah! Today: swipe to **List**, type, and send.

## 1x or Always

Every item is one of two kinds:

- **1x** (once): when you tick it, it's done for good. Good for "buy stamps" or "return the library book".
- **↻ Always**: ticking it means "I have it" or "I did it" for today. It comes back unticked tomorrow. Good for "laptop", "badge", "lunch".

Tap the **1x** or **↻ Always** tag next to an item to switch it.

## Set what new items start as

Under the box where you add items, choose **New items: 1x** or **↻ Always**. Your "Always take" list, for example, can start every new item as **↻ Always**.

## Put an item at a place

Tap **📍 Where?** next to an item and choose a place, such as the store or work. The item then shows up on Ah! Today when you're there. See [[places|Places]].

## Items with a ↺ reason

Sometimes an item appears with a small note such as **"↺ You buy this about every 9 days"**. That's [[helpers|Restock]] putting something back on your shopping list. Delete it if you don't need it, and Restock will hold off for a while.
