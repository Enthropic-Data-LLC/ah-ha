Ah-Ha keeps your lists, tasks, notes and day in one place, and helps you see the one thing that matters right now.

It is built to protect your attention: it shows you a little at a time, reminds you at the right moment, and stays quiet the rest of the time.

## Where to start

1. [[sign-in|Sign in]] at ah-ha.app with your email address.
2. Make a list or two, for example a shopping list.
3. Open [[ah-today|Ah! Today]] to see what matters now.
4. If you have an Android phone, [[install-android|install the apps]].

## The apps

- **Ah-Ha** on the web and on Android: your lists, tasks, notes and Ah! Today.
- **[[ah-rhythm|Ah! Rhythm]]**: supplements, medicines, walks, workouts and other things you do every day.
- **[[ah-cart|Ah! Cart]]**: a running total while you shop, so you know what you will pay before you reach the register.

Ah! Rhythm and Ah! Cart work on their own. Connect them to Ah-Ha and they share what they know with it.

> **Good to know:** The Android apps are in beta. They work, and we use them every day, but they are still changing.
