Ah-Ha has three helpers that work in the background. They never send you notifications of their own. They only add things where you'd look anyway (your lists, Ah! Today, your trail), and they always say why.

## Restock

Restock notices how often you buy things and puts them back on your shopping list when you're about to run out.

- It learns from items you tick off your shopping list, and from trips in [[ah-cart|Ah! Cart]].
- It waits until it has seen something bought on at least **three different days**.
- The item comes back with a note, for example **"↺ You buy this about every 9 days; last time 8 days ago"**.
- It adds **three items a day** at most, and never adds something that's already on your list.

**Don't need it?** Delete the item. Restock leaves that item alone for a good while before suggesting it again.

## Follow-through

Some things keep getting pushed back. Follow-through notices when you've snoozed something **three times** and helps you get started.

- Ah! Today brings the item forward, saying it's been **snoozed 3 times**.
- It suggests one **small first step** you could do in five minutes, for example "Find last year's tax return and put it on your desk."
- When you finish the item, the count starts again from zero.

Only snoozes **you** make count. The apps hiding things until their time (like Ah! Rhythm does) don't.

## Weekly patterns

Every **Sunday evening**, Weekly patterns writes a short note about your week to your trail, for example:

> 📈 Your week: Kept up: Fish oil 7/7 · Kept skipping: Evening walk skipped 3× · Put off: Call the plumber · Most done: mornings, Tuesdays

It looks at what you kept up, what you kept skipping, what you put off, and when you get the most done. Monday's briefing on Ah! Today uses it too.

## About the first steps

To write a first step, Follow-through sends the **title** of the item (only the title) to Anthropic's Claude. See [[your-data|Your data]].
