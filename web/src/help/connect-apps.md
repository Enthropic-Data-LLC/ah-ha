## Ah-Ha for Android

1. On a computer or your phone's browser, sign in at ah-ha.app and open **Keys**.
2. Make a new key and name it after your phone, for example "My phone".
3. Copy the key. It starts with **aha_**.
4. Open the Ah-Ha app and paste the key when it asks.

Keep the key private. Anyone who has it can open your Ah-Ha. If you lose your phone, delete the key on the **Keys** page.

## Ah! Rhythm and Ah! Cart

These two connect with one tap, no key needed:

1. Open the app's settings (⚙️ in Ah! Rhythm; **Pair with Ah-Ha** in Ah! Cart).
2. Tap **Connect**.
3. Your browser opens Ah-Ha. Check what the app is asking to do, then allow it.
4. You're sent back to the app, connected.

Each app only gets the access it needs. Ah! Cart, for example, can read your shopping list and tick items off, but it can't see your notes.

> **Good to know:** Both apps work fine without connecting. Connecting adds extras, like logging what you took to your Ah-Ha trail, or shopping straight from your Ah-Ha list.
